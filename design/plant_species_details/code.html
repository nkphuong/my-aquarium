Rotala Rotundifolia

Lythraceae • Southeast Asia

Stem Plant

description Description

Rotala Rotundifolia, often simply called "Rotala", is a classic aquarium stem plant that has been a staple in the hobby for decades. Originally from Southeast Asia, its Latin name "Rotundifolia" means "round-leaved", referring to its emersed growth form where the leaves are circular. However, once submerged, the leaves transform into long, narrow, and lanceolate shapes that flow beautifully in the current.

This plant is exceptionally versatile and relatively forgiving, making it an excellent choice for beginners looking to experiment with red plants. Under intense lighting and with sufficient iron supplementation, the tips of the stems will turn a vibrant pink to deep red. In lower light conditions, the plant remains a fresh lime green.

It is a fast grower and requires regular pruning to maintain a bushy appearance. Trimmings can be easily replanted to propagate the species, quickly filling out the background of your aquascape.
