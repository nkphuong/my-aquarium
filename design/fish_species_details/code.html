Description

The Betta Splendens, commonly known as the Siamese Fighting Fish, is one of the most popular freshwater aquarium fish. Native to the Mekong basin of Laos, Cambodia, Vietnam, and Thailand, these fish are renowned for their brilliant colors and flowing fins.

In the wild, Bettas inhabit standing waters of canals, rice paddies, and floodplains. They possess a unique labyrinth organ that allows them to breathe atmospheric air, an adaptation for surviving in oxygen-poor waters. Male Bettas are highly territorial and will flare their gill covers and spread their fins to intimidate rivals. They should never be housed with other male Bettas.
